Curie's Great Escape

Marc Greenberg wants Daniel Liu and Jordan Lamar on his solid phase PARP project forever. Curie Lee must climb NCB from the 1st to the 3rd floor, synthesize thiol-cholesterol, dodge classes and a mountain of orgo grading, then rescue her friends without Marc noticing. Each friend you save powers up your jump!

Arrows / A D to move, Space / W / ↑ to jump.

Tip: on Floor 3, stay off Marc's floor level.
